Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Summary: BowlisticS is completely offline. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal information or data.

1. Introduction

BowlisticS ("the App") is developed as a free, offline bowling scorekeeper and statistics application. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to protecting your privacy while using the App.

2. Information Collection and Use

BowlisticS does not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, address, phone number, email, or device identifiers). You can use all features of the App without creating an account or providing any personal details.

3. Local Data Storage

All bowling scores, frames, ball rolls, session logs, notes, and statistics you record are stored strictly on your local device in a private SQLite database. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or back it up yourself.

4. Database Backup & Restore

The App provides optional backup and restore functionality. When you create a backup, the database file is written directly to a folder or cloud drive (e.g., Google Drive) that you explicitly select using the standard Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework). We do not have access to your files, drives, or storage.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App contains no third-party tracking libraries, no analytics SDKs, and no advertisements. We do not track your usage, behavior, or device information.

6. Permissions

The App requests zero sensitive permissions. It does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions.

7. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
